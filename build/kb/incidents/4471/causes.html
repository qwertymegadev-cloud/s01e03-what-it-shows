How to tell them apart

  • parser: the shared parser reads every date month first, and every tenant on file writes the day first. The day and the month swap: 2 March becomes 3 February. It reaches every channel of every tenant.
  • hint: the booking-v3 form prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field, but reads what people type the tenant’s way, day first. People follow the hint, and the date swaps. Only reports that came in through the form, at a tenant on booking-v3. Fixing the parser does not fix this; the hint has to match what the form reads.
  • one-day move: the booking moves by one day, not by a swap. Tuesday becomes Monday, one client gets Tuesday while another in a different time zone gets Monday, an invite arrives before the booking was made. That looks like a time zone shift when the date is stored or sent, not a parser bug. The desk listed these reports in its notes, but no fix covers them.

Why one ticket hid three

  • Triage merges on the subject line (decision 0001). “Wrong date” fits all three problems, so all three landed in one ticket. Merging on subject alone needs a cause check before it.
  • The intake tag is a guess from the reporter’s browser, not from the tenant: the “tagged as” column shows how often it disagrees. Group by tenant, form and channel, as causes.ts does.
  • The table above already marked hint as “no ticket of its own” and footnoted the one-day moves. Status known made it read as finished.
